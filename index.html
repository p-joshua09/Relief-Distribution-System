      Counter workflow

      1. 1
        Register the beneficiary

        Enter beneficiary details, complete face and fingerprint enrollment, and save the registration.

      2. 2
        Verify identity and review claim history

        Verify the beneficiary, confirm their profile, and check whether they have received goods in the current batch.

      3. 3
        Confirm eligibility and hand over

        Review the current batch rules, confirm eligibility, record the actual goods and receipt acknowledgment, then issue the transaction confirmation. A declined handover records a reason and leaves no claim.

      Sign in, register new beneficiaries, and use Verification before distributing goods. Prepare a saved handover before giving goods and confirm receipt afterward. Pending handovers can be recovered after refresh.
